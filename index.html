Deep ITM

A personal trading journal and planning tool, used by one person.

Deep ITM is a private application its owner runs on their own computer to plan, record and review their own trades. It is not offered to the public, has no other users and no sign-up.

Privacy policy

Last updated 26 October 2026.

  • What it reads. With the owner's permission, Deep ITM reads the owner's own sleep and heart-rate data from Google (Fitbit) through the Google Health API.
  • Why. The data is used only inside the app, to help the owner decide how large their trades should be on a given day.
  • Where it is kept. The data is stored on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared, or sent to anyone else, and it is not used for advertising.
  • Who can see it. Only the owner.
  • Removing access. Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; the owner can delete the stored data from the app's settings.
